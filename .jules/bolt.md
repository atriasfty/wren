## 2025-02-20 - RAG Cosine Similarity Optimization
**Learning:** The `@xenova/transformers` library returns embeddings that are already unit-length normalized (`normalize: true`). Calculating the full cosine similarity for these embeddings involves redundantly recalculating the magnitudes (always ~1.0) and running expensive `Math.sqrt` operations per vector in the vector store.
**Action:** Always verify if vector embeddings are normalized. When comparing unit-normalized vectors, substitute full cosine similarity with a simple dot product to eliminate unnecessary arithmetic and square roots, drastically speeding up retrieval logic over large vector stores.
