# Wren

Wren is a friendly helper for your Discord server. It lives in your channels and answers questions about your rules, your community, and your players, the way a senior moderator would.

Wren was built for ERLC communities, but it works anywhere people need fast, accurate answers about how a server runs.

## Who Wren is for

- **Server owners** who want one place to point members when they ask "what's the rule on X?"
- **Moderators** who are tired of typing the same answer for the tenth time today
- **Communities** whose rules, handbooks, and FAQs live in too many places

## What you'll find here

The guide is split into three parts.

- **Getting Started** walks you from zero to a working bot in your server.
- **For Server Owners** covers everything you do once and then leave alone: setting up, giving Wren the rules, wiring up tickets.
- **For Moderators** is the everyday guide to chatting with Wren and using it to look up players.

If you want to look up a specific command, jump to the **Command Reference**.

## A taste of how it works

Drop a rulebook into your server, mention Wren, and ask:

> @Wren What happens if someone RDMs in my server?

Wren searches the rulebook you gave it, finds the right section, and answers in plain English. If Wren doesn't know, it says so instead of guessing.

You can also ask about your live server:

> @Wren Who's online right now?
> @Wren Has this player been banned before?
> @Wren Bring all staff to me.

Everything Wren does is gated by your permissions. If you haven't given a role the ability to ban players, Wren won't do it for them.
