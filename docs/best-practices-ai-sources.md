# Best Practices for Writing AI Sources

Wren's ability to answer questions accurately depends entirely on the quality of the sources you provide. If your rules are confusing, contradictory, or vague, Wren might get confused too. 

Here are the top tips for writing documents that an AI can easily understand.

---

## 1. Use Clear, Descriptive Headings
AI models rely heavily on headings to understand the context of a paragraph. Don't just use bold text; use Markdown headings (`#`, `##`, `###`) to structure your document.

**Avoid:**
**Ban Rules**
Don't exploit. Don't RDM.

**Do:**
### Server Rules regarding Bans
1. **Exploiting:** Using third-party software to gain an unfair advantage will result in a permanent ban.
2. **Random Deathmatch (RDM):** Killing players without a valid roleplay reason is strictly prohibited.

## 2. Avoid Ambiguous Pronouns
When a user asks a specific question, Wren pulls the most relevant snippet from your document. If that snippet uses words like "It", "They", or "This", Wren might not know what the pronoun refers to.

**Avoid:** "If you break it, you will be banned."
**Do:** "If a player breaks the Vehicle Spawn rule, the player will be banned."

## 3. Write in the Third Person
Instead of saying "We will ban you", say "The Moderation Team will ban the player". This helps Wren differentiate between its own identity (as an AI assistant) and the human staff members.

## 4. Use Q&A Formats
Wren loves Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) documents. If you find users asking the same question repeatedly, write it out exactly as they ask it, followed by the answer.

**Example:**
*Q: How do I apply for the Police Department?*
*A: To apply for the Police Department, you must be in the server for 7 days, reach level 10, and submit an application in the #pd-applications channel.*

## 5. Keep Information Centralized
Don't spread your rules across 15 different Discord channels. If you have conflicting information in two different sources, Wren might pick the wrong one. Keep your definitive rules in a single, well-maintained channel or document.
