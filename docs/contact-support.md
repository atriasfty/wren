# How to Contact Support

If you need help configuring Wren, want to appeal a Global Ban, or need assistance with billing, our support team is here to help!

You can reach us in two ways:

## 1. Join our Discord Server (Recommended)
The fastest way to get help is to join our official Discord community. You can open a ticket, ask questions in the community channels, and get live support from our team.

🔗 **[Join the Discord Server](https://atriasfty.org/discord)**

## 2. Email Us
If you prefer email, or if your query involves sensitive billing or privacy information, you can email our support desk.

✉️ **[help@atriasafety.org](mailto:help@atriasafety.org)**

---

*Note: If you are contacting us about a GDPR Data Erasure request, please contact our Data Protection Officer at [dpo@atriasafety.org](mailto:dpo@atriasafety.org) for the fastest processing.*
