# Welcome to Wren

Thanks for trying Wren. This short guide gets you from a fresh invite to a working bot in about five minutes.

## What you'll do

1. Invite Wren to your Discord server.
2. Tell Wren it's allowed to be there with one command.
3. Point Wren at the places where your rules and guides live.
4. Ask your first question.

You don't need to touch any code. Wren is configured entirely from inside Discord with slash commands.

## What you need

- **Admin or Manage Server** permission on the Discord server.
- **Five minutes** for the first setup.
- **Your server's ERLC key**, if you want Wren to be able to talk to your in-game server.

That's it. Wren handles the rest.
