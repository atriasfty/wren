# What Wren Does

Wren has three jobs. Once you understand them, every other guide in this book makes sense.

## Job 1: Answer questions about your community

Wren reads the things you tell it to read and answers in plain English. If a member asks "what's the rule on mass RDM?" Wren searches your rulebook, finds the right section, and replies in the same channel.

The things Wren can read are called **sources of truth**. They can be:

- A Discord channel where your rules are pinned
- A website that hosts your handbook
- A document you paste in directly

You can mix and match. A typical setup has one Discord channel, one website, and a couple of short documents.

## Job 2: Look up what's happening on your server

Wren can also talk to your in-game server. It can answer questions like:

- Who's online right now?
- Is this player staff?
- Has this player been punished recently?

If you want, Wren can also take action for you. Moderators can ask it to kick, ban, teleport, message, or warn a player without ever opening the game panel. Wren will refuse to do anything your team hasn't given them permission to do.

## Job 3: Keep an eye on things in the background

While nobody's talking to it, Wren watches for two patterns:

- **Tickets** — when someone opens a ticket, Wren posts a friendly hello so they know a human is on the way.
- **Raids** — when one player suddenly runs a flood of commands, Wren raises an alert. If you want, Wren will auto-ban the offending player.

Both are optional. Turn them on when you're ready.

## What Wren is not

Wren is not a replacement for human moderators. It is a force multiplier. It does the typing, the searching, and the looking-up so your team can spend more time on the calls that need a person.
