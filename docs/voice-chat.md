# Voice Chat 🎙️

Wren is not just a text assistant—you can now speak to Wren directly in your Discord Voice Channels!

Voice Chat is built to be fast, conversational, and incredibly helpful. You can ask questions, run commands, and get answers out loud, completely hands-free.

---

## How to Invite Wren

You can summon Wren to your voice channel at any time:

1. Join a Voice Channel in your server.
2. Type `/wren voice join` in any text channel.
3. Wren will immediately join your voice channel.

To make Wren leave, simply type `/wren voice leave`, or wait for Wren to automatically disconnect if the channel becomes empty.

---

## Voice Chat Limits & Billing

To keep the service fast and responsive, Voice Chat usage is limited based on your server's subscription tier. Usage is calculated based on "active time"—the time between when Wren hears the wake word (the upward charm) and when you finish speaking your prompt (the downward charm).

- **Free Tier**: 2 Voice Minutes per month
- **Core Tier**: 30 Voice Minutes per month
- **Pro Tier**: 120 Voice Minutes per month

Once your server exhausts its voice minutes, Wren will silently ignore the wake word until your billing cycle resets. You can view your current usage at any time with `/wren manage` or `$atria serverinfo`.

---

## Terms of Service & Privacy

Before Wren can listen to you, you must consent to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

The first time you try to speak to Wren in a voice channel, Wren will play an auditory warning stating that you have not accepted the Terms of Service. A direct message (DM) will be sent to your Discord account with a link to accept the terms.

Once you click **"Agree"**, you can return to the voice channel and immediately start talking. You only need to do this once.

> [!NOTE]
> For privacy reasons, Wren does **not** record or store your voice messages. Audio is processed dynamically in real-time, converted to text for the AI, and then immediately discarded.

---

## How to Talk to Wren

Wren operates using a **"Wake Word"** system, similar to a smart home device. Wren is not constantly listening to everything you say; it only pays attention when you explicitly call its name.

### 1. Say "Hey Wren"
To get Wren's attention, clearly say **"Hey Wren"**.

### 2. Wait for the Charm
When Wren hears you, it will play a pleasant ascending charm sound (🎵). This indicates that Wren is now actively listening to your request.

### 3. Ask your Question
After you hear the charm, state your request clearly. You can ask anything from "What is the server IP?" to "Can you check my punishment history?".

### 4. Wait for Processing
When you stop speaking, Wren will play a confirmation chime to let you know it got your request. It will think for a second, and then speak the response aloud using its high-quality voice!
